## What my assistants handle

I run a set of AI assistants for my own life and work. Each owns one area, works from real records instead of guesses, and asks before it changes a record or sends anything on my behalf.

- **Training:** syncs my coach's planned workouts, registers me for weekly races, and debriefs each session with what the data does and does not support.
- **Food:** turns meal photos, labels and receipts into a nutrition and cost log, and suggests what to eat around training.
- **Studies:** a morning and evening digest of new course material, grades and exam deadlines, plus maths and CS tutoring that finds the first error in my own attempt.
- **Admin and money:** keeps finances, taxes, paperwork and deadlines in view, so nothing comes due unnoticed.
- **Coaching business:** takes applications from my website, keeps one record per client, and drafts programs, emails and invoices for my approval.
- **Research:** weekly scans of my field and of the market and AI accounts I follow, background checks on people and brands before I reach out, and evidence tests for product ideas before I build them.
- **Home and code:** lights and air conditioning on request, and agreed code changes delivered as verified patches.
